Privacy Notice & Community Rules

NOTICE: This document is an internal operational draft marked strictly for review by Ikaai Legal or HR. It does not constitute binding statutory representation.

1. Purpose of Data Collection

Ikaai India Foundation collects and processes personal information (such as full name, email address, contact phone number, module completion milestones, and examination answers) exclusively to manage internship learning cohorts, record evaluations, issue authentic certificates of completion, and coordinate research deliverables.

2. Contact & Data Protection Officer

For data queries, access requests, or corrections, contact our designated officer:
Data Protection Officer: [DATA PROTECTION OFFICER PLACEHOLDER: dpo@ikaaiindia.org]
Organizational Contact: [IKAAI CONTACT PLACEHOLDER: hr@ikaaiindia.in / Registered Office: New Delhi]

3. Data Retention Period

User account data, assessment logs, and submitted examinations are retained for:
[RETENTION PERIOD PLACEHOLDER: Duration of active internship plus 12 calendar months for academic verification], after which records are archived or anonymized.

4. Community Rules & Workplace Conduct

  • Zero Harassment: We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy towards harassment, discrimination, or abusive language in chat threads or messaging.
  • Data Confidentiality: Field research survey datasets, beneficiary records, and proprietary organizational proposals must not be exported or distributed outside authorized Foundation channels.
  • Academic Integrity: Quizzes and written assessments must reflect individual intern analysis. Plagiarism or sharing answer sheets is strictly prohibited.
